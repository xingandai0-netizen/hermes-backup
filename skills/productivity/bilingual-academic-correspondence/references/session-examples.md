# Session Examples — Bilingual Correspondence

## Example 1: Leave of Absence Email (Stanfort University, Singapore)

**Context:** Student needs to request temporary leave due to family reasons. Professor (Dr Wang Chaochen) explicitly requested bilingual format.

**User's original (Chinese only):**
```
Chaochen老师您好：
我是您的学生戴幸安。由于家庭原因，我需要申请暂时休学...
```

**Professor's response:**
> Please write in both Chinese and English because the official language in Singapore and Great Britain is English.

**Final bilingual version:**
```
Subject: Leave of Absence Request — MSc-November 2025

Dear Dr Wang Chaochen,

Thank you for your prompt response. Please find my request below in both Chinese and English.

申请暂时休学 / Application for Temporary Leave of Absence

我是您的学生戴幸安（学号：25028666），课程：MSc-IAF - November 2025。
由于家庭原因，我需要申请暂时休学。

I am your student Dai Xingan (Student ID: 25028666), enrolled in MSc-IAF - November 2025. Due to family reasons, I am writing to request a temporary leave of absence.

事由 / Reason：
因家中突发变故，目前无法继续正常学业，需要暂时中断学业回国处理家中事务。待家中情况稳定后，我会尽快申请复学。

Due to sudden family circumstances, I am unable to continue my studies at this time and need to return home to attend to family matters. I plan to apply for reinstatement once the situation stabilises.

请教事宜 / Matters I would like to enquire about：
1. 办理暂时休学的具体流程和所需材料
   The specific process and required documents for filing a leave of absence.
2. 当前课程如何处理（是否可以保留学分或暂缓考核）
   How the current coursework will be handled (whether credits can be retained or assessments deferred).
3. 休学期间是否需要注意的事项
   Any matters I should be aware of during the leave period.

给您带来的不便深表歉意，希望能得到您的理解与支持。感谢您的时间，期待您的回复。
I sincerely apologise for any inconvenience and hope for your understanding and support. Thank you for your time, and I look forward to your reply.

Best regards,
戴幸安 Dai Xingan
Student ID: 25028666
Email: 18957167833@163.com
Phone: +86 83758578
```

**Lesson:** Always default to bilingual for Singapore institutions. User wrote in Chinese, professor had to ask for English. Proactively offer bilingual next time.

## Example 2: Rental Contract Inquiry (Singapore Company)

**Context:** Tenant wants to propose replacement tenant due to inability to move in.

```
Subject: Tenancy Agreement 8418-804048-CR4 — Unable to Move In & Request for Replacement Tenant

Dear BESPOKE HABITAT Client Care Team,

My name is DAI XINGAN, and I am the tenant under Tenancy Agreement 8418-804048-CR4 (Room CR4, PARC OASIS, 41 Jurong East Avenue 1, #04-04), with a fixed term from 24 Jul 2026 to 23 Oct 2026.

I am writing to inform you that due to personal reasons, I will be unable to move into the premises as scheduled.

I have reviewed the Tenancy Agreement and understand that under Clause 5.3, I may propose a replacement tenant to take over the remaining lease period. I would like to enquire about the following:

1. Whether I am permitted to source a replacement tenant at this stage (prior to the lease start date);
2. The specific process and requirements for the replacement tenant to sign a new agreement;
3. Whether the Security Deposit of S$1,400.00 can be refunded upon successful replacement, as stipulated in Clause 5.3(D)-(F);
4. Whether there are any additional steps or documentation required from my end.

I have already paid the Security Deposit, first month's rent, and all one-time fees totalling S$2,986.00. I would appreciate your guidance on how to proceed so that we can resolve this matter as smoothly as possible.

Best regards,
DAI XINGAN
Identity No.: M0524975Q
```

**Lesson:** For Singapore companies, English-only is standard. Reference specific contract clauses to show due diligence.

## Example 3: Medical Refund Request (Singapore Airlines)

**Context:** Passenger needs medical refund for flight booked through Ctrip.

```
Subject: Medical Refund Request — SQ839 / Booking E86NIH

Dear Singapore Airlines,

I am writing to request a full refund for my booking due to medical reasons.

Booking Reference: E86NIH
E-ticket Number: 618-2479727614
Passenger Name: DAI XINGAN
Flight: SQ839, Hangzhou to Singapore, 6 August 2026

I was recently diagnosed with an anxiety disorder at Jinhua Second Hospital on 4 August 2026. My medical examination revealed:
1. Recurrent chest tightness and breathing difficulties under stress
2. HAMA anxiety scale: confirmed anxiety symptoms
3. ECG results: sinus bradycardia with arrhythmia, right axis deviation

My doctor has advised against travel at this time. I have attached the hospital outpatient medical record for your reference.

I kindly request a full refund of CNY 1,191.00 under your medical circumstances policy.

Best regards,
DAI XINGAN
```

**Pitfall discovered:** Third-party bookings (Ctrip/携程) CANNOT be refunded directly with the airline. Must go through the original booking platform. Always check booking channel first before advising on refund process.
