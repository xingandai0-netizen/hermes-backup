## Schedule Structure Notes (from Day 8 session)
- 48-team format = 12 groups × 4 teams = 48 group-stage matches per round × 3 rounds
- Round 2 matches within the same group can be split across DIFFERENT days (e.g. Group A: CZE-RSA on June 19 00:00 Beijing, but MEX-KOR possibly on June 20 or different time slot)
- When user asks "明天3点的比赛", they may expect ALL Round 2 matches from their groups of interest, not just the ones at exactly 3am — always confirm which specific matches they want

## Time Zone Reference
- **US Eastern (ET)**: Beijing time - 12 hours
- **US Central (CT)**: Beijing time - 13 hours
- **US Pacific (PT)**: Beijing time - 15 hours
- **UK (BST)**: Beijing time - 7 hours
- Example: 3:00 PM ET = 3:00 AM Beijing time (next day)
