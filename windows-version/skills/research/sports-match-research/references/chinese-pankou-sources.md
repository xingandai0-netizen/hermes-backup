## Key Patterns

1. **DuckDuckGo rate limits on Chinese searches** — Chinese-language queries are especially prone to timeouts. Limit to 3-4 searches per topic.

2. **Platform-specific search** — Adding site: prefix helps:
   - `site:baxi.tv 世界杯 推荐`
   - `site:168.tips 盘口分析`

3. **Time-sensitive** — Chinese analysis platforms update closer to match time (6-12 hours before). Search results for "6月17日" queries work best on match day itself.

4. **Format** — Chinese analysts typically provide:
   - 让球盘 (Asian Handicap)
   - 大小球 (Over/Under)
   - 胜平负 (1X2)
   - 比分预测 (Correct Score)
   - 串关推荐 (Parlay/Accumulator)
