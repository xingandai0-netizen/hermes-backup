# Landing Page Research (2026-07-03)

## 5 Mainstream AI Canvas Tool Homepages

### TapNow.ai
- Hero: "Your Agentic Creative Canvas"
- Style: Dark, cinematic, interactive demos
- Social proof: Alibaba, Google Ads, TikTok, YouTube logos
- CTA: "Get started for free"

### Krea.ai
- Hero: "AI Creative Suite for Images, Video & 3D"
- Stats: 30M+ users, 191 countries
- Pricing: 6 tiers (Free $0 → Enterprise custom)
- Backed by: a16z, BCV

### Midjourney
- Style: Minimalist, philosophical, research lab manifesto
- No traditional SaaS page — relies on Discord community
- Differentiation: brand-building over conversion

### RunwayML
- Hero: "Building AI to Simulate the World"
- Focus: Research-forward, enterprise-grade
- Multiple "Learn more" CTAs, not aggressive conversion

### Canva
- Hero: "Visual Suite for Everyone"
- Style: Light/bright, colorful, mass-market
- Heavy product screenshots

## Recommended Landing Page Structure (12 blocks)
1. Navbar (sticky + CTA)
2. Hero (headline + sub + CTA + product demo)
3. Social Proof Logos
4. Features (3-4 key features, alternating layout)
5. Product Showcase (interactive demo)
6. AI Models (logo grid)
7. Use Cases (3-step flow)
8. Pricing Table
9. Testimonials
10. FAQ
11. Final CTA
12. Footer

## Design Patterns
- Dark theme dominant for AI tools
- Specific CTAs ("Start free trial" > "Get started")
- Interactive demos > static screenshots
- Model logos/grid showing supported AI models
- "Try it free" as primary conversion
